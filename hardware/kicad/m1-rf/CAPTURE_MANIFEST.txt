POR10 M1 RF schematic capture manifest
======================================

Receiver: Si4735-D60-GU/GUR family, 24-pin SSOP
Controller during M1: external ESP32-S3 development harness
Primary control: I2C
RF inputs: separate FMI and AMI prototype paths
Audio: analog L/R breakout
Baseline: no LNA, no filter bank, no automatic antenna switch
